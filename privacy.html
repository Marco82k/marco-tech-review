Privacy Policy

Marco Tech Studio · last updated 5 November 2026

Marco Tech Studio is a personal tool made and used only by Marco Strusi to prepare his own product review videos and send them to his own social media accounts (TikTok, YouTube and Instagram). It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the app uses

When Marco connects one of his accounts, the platform gives the app an access token and basic profile information (such as the account name and avatar). The app uses them only to upload Marco's own videos, with their captions and hashtags, to that same account, and to check the status of those uploads.

Where the data is kept

Access tokens and videos are stored only on Marco's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than the official APIs of TikTok, YouTube (Google) and Instagram (Meta), and only to perform the actions described above.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, does not show ads and does not track anyone.

Data of other people

The app does not collect or process data of other users, followers or viewers.

Deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from the security or connected-apps settings of each platform. Revoking it, or deleting the local app folder, removes all stored tokens.
